The daily cycle

Five states, one lap a day

The bot is a state machine anchored to UTC midnight. It cannot enter outside its slots, cannot exceed its heat, and cannot end a day without writing down what happened.

Briefing

Pull bars, score the news per ticker and per coin, rank the universe cross-sectionally, and measure each alt's beta to BTC.

Entry

Crypto at four-hour boundaries, equities inside the US session and clear of the opening auction. Size is risk ÷ (k × ATR), never a fixed dollar amount.

Manage

Trail the stop, scale out at R rungs, pyramid into a winner at half size. Correlation and net-beta caps apply per risk factor, not per symbol.

Flatten

Hit the daily loss limit and the day ends early — positions closed, no new entries. The limit is not advisory.

Report

Per-day, per-class and per-symbol P&L with expectancy in R, a standard error and a t-stat against the right critical value.

Publish

State lands in JSON that this site reads. Stale data turns the indicator amber rather than showing old numbers as current.

Your keys, your accounts

Alpaca

Paper and live US equities and ETFs. Bars at 15m, 1h and 1d, with paging past the 10,000-bar cap.

Crypto venues

News
